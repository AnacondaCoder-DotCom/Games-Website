# Games-Website
Hopefully soon to be a Website with minesweeper, sudoku, and other fun and simple games that are available on my school laptop. To start off the first game is Minesweeper. To play simply select a difficulty and then start to click and reveal squares. Once a square is revealed and it is not a mine it will show numbers in the squares surrounding it, which show up because they have proximity to a mine. The numbers show how many mines are in the 8 un-revealed squares surrounding that square. If you press a square and the color simply shifts and no numbers show up close to it that means that you have hit a string of non-mine square, which isn't a bug, it's just rare. Finally, press right click to place a flag on potential mines. It will prevent you from clicking on that square until the mine is remove. Once all mines have been flagged and all squares revealed you win!
